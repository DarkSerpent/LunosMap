Shadow Ball is deceptively lethal because it punishes both caution and aggression at the same time: a Ghost-type attack with consistent base power, wide distribution, and a built-in chance to lower Special Defense. It bypasses the defensive comfort of Normal- and Fighting-type cores, pressures Psychic and Ghost threats instantly, and allows fast special attackers to snowball. Don't underestimate it.
