O guardian spirits of snow and frost, In Flourfall's embrace, where dreams are tossed. Beneath the snowy veil, your presence clear, We gather here at this shrine, drawing near. In the quiet of the snowy night, Guide us with your gentle, shimmering light. To the trainers and Pokemon who roam, Bless their journey as they find a home.
